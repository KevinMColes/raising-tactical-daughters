The signature topic

Raising Her Ready.

What to teach your daughter, age by age.

Parents cannot stand beside their daughters through every message, friendship, car ride, or difficult decision. They can build the skills and the relationship that help when they are not there.

Drawing on the RTD guide, Kevin M. Coles leads a conversation about practical preparation through each stage of growing up, from early boundaries to the first phone and greater independence.

The focus is on everyday choices, useful language, and actions families can practice together.

The workplace topic

Ready at Work.

Personal safety and harassment prevention training for employees.

Every company has a harassment policy. Most employees still say nothing when something happens, because they have seen what happens to the person who speaks up. Ready at Work covers the part between the policy and the moment.

Drawing on the free Ready at Work guide, Kevin M. Coles leads workplace safety training on recognizing harassment early, setting boundaries with coworkers, managers, and clients, documenting and reporting without fear of retaliation, staying safe during business travel, events, and late exits, and what to do when domestic violence or stalking follows someone to work.

Formats

  • 45-minute keynote for all-hands meetings and kickoffs
  • 90-minute scenario-based workshop
  • Women's employee resource group (ERG) session
  • Manager session on receiving reports and preventing retaliation, included with every booking

In person in Arizona or virtual. This is awareness and confidence training. It is not compliance training or legal advice, and it does not replace your harassment policy or workplace violence prevention plan.

What every session covers

Three ideas. Every stage of life.

01

Trust your gut.

The uneasy feeling is information. Daughters and employees alike learn to act on it before they can prove anything.

02

Use your voice.

Boundaries, asking for help, and reporting. Kindness and professionalism never require compliance.

03

You can always leave.

The party, the car, the meeting, the client dinner. Practice the exit before the pressure arrives.
