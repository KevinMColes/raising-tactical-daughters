The signature topic

Raising Her Ready.

What to teach your daughter, age by age.

Parents cannot stand beside their daughters through every message, friendship, car ride, or difficult decision. They can build the skills—and the relationship—that help when they are not there.

Drawing on the RTD guide, Kevin M. Coles leads a conversation about practical preparation through each stage of growing up, from early boundaries to the first phone and greater independence.

The focus is on everyday choices, useful language, and actions families can practice together.

What the conversation covers

Useful beyond the room.

01

Give her a voice.

Boundaries, asking for help, and teaching daughters that kindness does not require compliance.

02

Practice the decision.

Phones, sleepovers, parties, and rides: talk through the moment before the pressure arrives.

03

Keep the door open.

Responding in a way that makes it easier for a daughter to ask for help when something goes wrong.

Your speaker

Kevin M. Coles.

Kevin M. Coles founded Raising Tactical Daughters in 2019, inspired by the birth of his first daughter. He is now a father of two daughters, an author, and a technology leader.

He has spoken to Salt River Project’s Women’s Group in a session that included discussion and audience questions. RTD’s story has appeared in ABC15 Arizona, VoyagePhoenix, and Peoria Independent.
