More than a shirt

A way to say:
she deserves to be ready.

A shirt made the idea visible. The purpose behind it was practical: support preparation, start conversations, and help women and girls build confidence in their own decisions.

Proceeds supported in-person and online training for women. Families shared the message. First responders and service members carried the shield into their own communities.

The reach matters because of the people behind it. The photographs and reflections here are part of that history.
