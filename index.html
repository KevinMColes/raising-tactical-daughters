It started
with his
first daughter.

Kevin M. Coles founded Raising Tactical Daughters in 2019. What began with becoming a father grew into a community around a shared belief: girls deserve the confidence and preparation to move through the world.

Today, that responsibility includes both of his daughters—and the families this resource is here to support.
